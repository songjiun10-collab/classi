autoresearch trains a tiny model under a fixed wall-clock budget and measures validation bits per byte.
the budget is fixed so different code changes are compared at equal compute, not at equal effort.
the metric is per byte so changing the vocabulary cannot game the score.
prepare stays frozen during a search; only the search space in train may change; the loop keeps whatever lowers val_bpb.
hill climbing over hyperparameters finds coupled optima that a single change could not reveal on its own.
the same scientific loop applies everywhere: fix the metric, fix the budget, iterate, keep the best.
